Career Reports

A Career Report maps what you enter onto curated occupations and courses. It is not written by a paid language model. Salary and employment figures are not copied into the report. Speculative future roles stay labeled Speculative — not a job promise.

Role pages

These are the occupations a Career Report can recommend. Each page is that role’s summary from the same curated list. Speculative sketches stay inside a Career Report. They do not have a page here.
